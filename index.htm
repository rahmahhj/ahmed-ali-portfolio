I am Ahmed Ali, an interior designer based in Duhok, Iraq. I believe great design goes beyond appearance — it creates comfort, enhances well-being, and reflects the personality of the people who live in it.

My approach combines thoughtful layouts, beautiful materials, lighting, and details to create spaces that feel both timeless and personal.
